# Vulkan feature backlog (work packages)

Derived from `vk/FEATURE-MATRIX-VULKAN.md` and `tools/feature-matrix/data/gaps.json` (both generated by
`tools/feature-matrix/feature_matrix.py`; regenerate after every package). Every "ours" value below comes from the
fake-backend `vulkaninfo --json` captures in `tools/feature-matrix/data/ours-v1x.json`, which match the phones'
vulkaninfo apart from three node- or layer-dependent items (see the matrix). Each "required by" entry has a citation
in `gaps.json`: a profile JSON key, `zink.rst:<line>` or `vk_renderer.cpp:<line>`. DXVK and vkd3d-proton needs are in
`vk/FEATURE-MATRIX-DIRECTX.md`, written by the DirectX lane; this file names them only as consumers.

Consumer verdicts that follow from the data:
- **ANGLE (Chromium 126): capped at ES 3.0 on all three GPUs.** ES 3.1 is blocked by
  `maxPerStageDescriptorStorageBuffers` = 4 (ANGLE needs 12: vk_renderer.cpp:3807-3812, Constants.h:88,118).
- **zink: blocked at the GL 2.1 requirement list.** The missing items are `alphaToOne`, the
  stippled and smooth line modes, and `VK_EXT_border_color_swizzle`. After that, GL 3.0 needs
  `VK_EXT_conditional_rendering`, GL 3.2 needs `geometryShader`, GL 4.0 `tessellationShader`, GL 4.2
  `vertexPipelineStoresAndAtomics` and `VK_EXT_image_2d_view_of_3d`, GL 4.5 `shaderCullDistance`, and GL 4.6
  `drawIndirectCount`. zink.rst says that not all of these are verified, so the GL version zink really exposes on
  our driver is a HYPOTHESIS. The deciding test is `glxinfo -B` under zink in the guest (phone lane).
- **Vulkan core conformance holes.** We report API 1.3, but a 1.1/1.2 device must support `multiview`
  (`maxMultiviewViewCount` >= 6) and `VK_RESOLVE_MODE_SAMPLE_ZERO_BIT` depth/stencil resolve. Ours reports
  `multiview` false, view count 0 and empty resolve modes. Cite: Vulkan-Profiles
  `VP_LUNARG_minimum_requirements_1_1/1_2`, keys `vulkan11requirements` and `vulkan12requirements`.
- **Arm's own driver shows what the hardware can do.** It reports, for all three GPUs: 8 colour attachments,
  1024 compute invocations, 32 KiB shared memory, point size 1-1024, `subTexelPrecisionBits` 8,
  `mipmapPrecisionBits` 8, LOD bias 126, `timestampComputeAndGraphics`, ETC2 and ASTC. It also reports
  `geometryShader` and `tessellationShader`. That is emulation, because Valhall has no such stages. Sources: gpuinfo
  #53324, #53727, #51936.

## Rules for every package
- Advertise only what a test backs (mali-open skill). The `feature-matrix vX` rows in `vk/check.sh` fail when an
  advertised item disappears. When a package adds an item, re-capture the three GPUs (`feature_matrix.py ours`) and run
  `gen` in the same commit, so that the new item is guarded from then on.
- Hot files are `mali_cmd.c`, `mali_cs_emit.c`, `mali_pipeline.c` and `mali_wsi.c`. Only one package at a time may
  touch them, and the orchestrator assigns it. Each package below says whether it needs them (**HOT**).
- Test plan notation: `t_*` is a host test on the fake backend and gates the merge. dEQP-VK groups run through the
  `vk/deqp` runner (PR #391, `vk/deqp/README.md`), on the host where it can and on a phone in a lane. Confirm group
  names against the case list (`vk/deqp/gen-caselists.py`) before you rely on them.
- Priority is 1-10, where 10 means the most real-application benefit today per day of work. The weights are the
  product path first (Steam client, DXVK and zink games, ANGLE/CEF), then the profiles.

## The first ten (in this order)

### WP-01 Descriptor and buffer limits up to roadmap-2022 values (priority 10)
- **What:** raise the following limits.
  - Per-stage limits:
    - storage buffers: 4 -> at least 30
    - sampled images: 16 -> at least 200
    - samplers: 16 -> at least 64
    - storage images: 4 -> at least 16
    - input attachments: 4 -> at least 7
  - Set-wide limits:
    - sampled images: at least 1800
    - samplers: at least 576
    - storage buffers: at least 96
    - storage images: at least 144
    - uniform buffers: at least 155
  - Other limits:
    - `maxPerStageResources`: at least 200
    - `maxFragmentCombinedOutputResources`: at least 16
    - `maxUniformBufferRange`: 16384 -> 65536
    - `maxStorageBufferRange`: 128 MiB -> 1 GiB
    - `maxTexelBufferElements`: 65536 -> 64 Mi
  - Also check `maxMemoryAllocationSize` (1 GiB -> at least 2 GiB, LDB24).
  - All values are in the matrix limits table, with the Arm blob values next to them.
- **Files:**
  - `vk/src/mali_physical_device.c` (limits)
  - `mali_descriptor.c`, `mali_descriptor_lower.c`, `mali_pipeline_layout*.c` (table sizes, resource-table packing)
  - `mali_buffer.c` (texel buffer)
- **Hardware:** Valhall resource tables are memory-backed and indexed, so these are driver choices, not hardware
  caps. The Arm blob reports 500000 and more on v11/v13 and 8388608 on v10. The UBO range is limited by how we
  push UBOs (FAU and resource table): 64 KiB is the roadmap value and is safe.
- **Consumers:** ANGLE ES 3.1 (blocked today), DXVK D3D11 (128 SRV, 8 or 64 UAV; see the DirectX list), roadmap 2022
  and 2024, Android 15/16, LDB24, Unity (UNITY-GAPS.md).
- **Depends on:** nothing. It is a prerequisite of WP-08.
- **Tests:**
  - Extend `t_vk_info` to assert the new limits.
  - Add a `t_desc` case that binds the maximum count per stage and draws.
  - dEQP-VK: `binding_model.descriptor_count_limits.*` (if present in the case list), `binding_model.shader_access.*`,
    `api.info.device.properties`.
- **Size:** 2 days. Not HOT, unless the resource-table emit lives in `mali_cs_emit.c`. Check that first.

### WP-02 Eight colour attachments (priority 9)
- **What:** `maxColorAttachments` 4 -> 8, `maxFragmentOutputAttachments` 4 -> 8, and blend state for 8 RTs.
- **Files:** `mali_physical_device.c`, `mali_render.c`, `mali_blend.c`, and the RT descriptors in
  `mali_cmd.c`/`mali_cs_emit.c` (**HOT**), plus the tile-buffer budget check.
- **Hardware:** Valhall has 8 render targets (the Arm blob reports 8 on all three GPUs). The tile-buffer bytes per
  pixel limit the tile size: with 8 RTs and MSAA the tile must shrink. PanVK does this in its tile-size selection;
  read it as an oracle and do not copy it.
- **Consumers:** DXVK D3D11/D3D10 (8 RTVs: deferred renderers), zink (GL `MAX_DRAW_BUFFERS` 8), roadmap 2022 (7),
  1.4 minimum (8), LDB24.
- **Depends on:** nothing.
- **Tests:**
  - `t_vk_draw` case with 8 attachments, plus a golden CS (`tests/golden`).
  - dEQP-VK: `renderpass.*.attachment.*` with 8 attachments, `pipeline.monolithic.blend.*`, `draw.*` (MRT).
- **Size:** 2-3 days.

### WP-03 Multiview (Vulkan 1.1 conformance hole) (priority 8)
- **What:**
  - `multiview` = true, `maxMultiviewViewCount` >= 6, `maxMultiviewInstanceIndex` >= 2^27-1.
  - Advertise `VK_KHR_multiview`.
  - Make the `ViewIndex` builtin work with a render-pass view mask.
- **Files:** `mali_render.c`, `mali_cmd.c` (**HOT**: replay the render pass per view, each into its own layer),
  `mali_compile.c` (lower `ViewIndex` to a push or sysval).
- **Hardware:** Valhall has no multiview hardware, so the driver replays the pass once per view (this is the PanVK
  approach: `multiview` is true there on v10/v11/v13). The Arm blob reports 8 views.
- **Consumers:**
  - Vulkan 1.1-1.4 core requirement (`VP_LUNARG_minimum_requirements_1_1`).
  - Android baseline 2022 and A15/A16.
  - LDB24.
  - ANGLE (`OVR_multiview`).
  - VR (Quest-class apps).
- **Depends on:** nothing.
- **Tests:** `t_vk_feat multiview` (fake: N passes recorded, layer per view). dEQP-VK: `multiview.*`
  (`multiview.masks.*` first).
- **Size:** 3 days.

### WP-04 ETC2 and ASTC LDR sampling (priority 8)
- **What:** `textureCompressionETC2` and `textureCompressionASTC_LDR` (both false today), with SAMPLED, FILTER_LINEAR
  and BLIT_SRC on every ETC2/EAC/ASTC LDR format. The matrix formats table lists 28 ASTC/ETC rows marked MISS.
- **Files:** `mali_format.c`, `mali_image.c` (block-compressed layout), `mali_sampler.c`.
- **Hardware:** Valhall decodes ETC2, EAC and ASTC LDR natively. The Arm blob advertises both on all three GPUs.
  PanVK queries the GPU's texture-feature registers (`has_texture_compression_*`, panvk_vX_physical_device.c), so
  read the kbase GPU properties and do not hard-code: X4, CMF and X8 may differ in ASTC HDR. Note: BC is emulated
  today (`mali_bc.c`); that path stays.
- **Consumers:** Android baseline 2021/2022 and A15/A16 (ETC2 or ASTC is mandatory), ANGLE (ES 3.0 requires ETC2;
  without it ANGLE decodes on the CPU), Unity Android-built assets.
- **Depends on:** nothing.
- **Tests:** a `t_image` format-property case, plus a `t_vk_feat` sample case (pixels on hardware). dEQP-VK:
  `texture.compressed.*`, `api.info.format_properties.*`, `image.astc_decode_mode.*` (with WP-21).
- **Size:** 2 days.

### WP-05 zink GL 2.1/3.0 baseline: line modes, alphaToOne, border swizzle, conditional rendering (priority 8)
- **What:**
  - `VK_EXT_border_color_swizzle`.
  - `alphaToOne`.
  - The `smoothLines`, `stippledRectangularLines`, `stippledBresenhamLines` and `stippledSmoothLines` features of
    `VK_KHR/EXT_line_rasterization`.
  - `VK_EXT_conditional_rendering` (zink GL 3.0).
- **Files:**
  - `mali_sampler.c` (border swizzle)
  - `mali_blend.c` (alphaToOne: force the alpha output to 1 in the blend shader or the fixed-function equation)
  - `mali_compile.c` (line stipple as a fragment discard on a line-distance varying)
  - `mali_cmd.c` (**HOT**: conditional rendering skips draws and dispatches on a predicate; CSF can predicate with a
    CS `load + branch` on the buffer value)
- **Hardware:**

  - Conditional rendering: PanVK v10+ through CSF command-stream branches.
  - Stipple and smooth lines: neither PanVK nor the blob has them, so they are shader emulation (smooth lines are an
    approximation: coverage from distance).
  - alphaToOne: blend-shader emulation.
- **Consumers:** zink GL 2.1 list (zink.rst:31-76), GL 3.0 (zink.rst:77-91), roadmap 2024 (line features), Android 15
  (one of Bresenham or software lines), ANGLE.
- **Depends on:** nothing. Splitting is possible: 05a border swizzle + alphaToOne (1 day), 05b line stipple/smooth
  (2 days), 05c conditional rendering (2 days, **HOT**).
- **Tests:** `t_vk_feat` per feature. dEQP-VK: `conditional_rendering.*`, `rasterization.primitives.*stipple*`,
  `rasterization.primitives.*smooth*`, `pipeline.monolithic.sampler.border_swizzle.*`,
  `pipeline.monolithic.multisample.alpha_to_one.*`.

### WP-06 Depth/stencil resolve SAMPLE_ZERO and MSAA completeness (priority 7)
- **What:**
  - `supportedDepthResolveModes` and `supportedStencilResolveModes` must contain SAMPLE_ZERO (Vulkan 1.2 core); add
    MIN/MAX and `independentResolveNone` (LDB24).
  - Finish MSAA LOAD + STORE + resolve. That combination is refused today in `mali_cmd.c` (see `vk/src/mali_msaa.h`),
    while 4x is now advertised by default.
  - 2x and 8x sample counts, integer MSAA sampling.
  - `VK_EXT_multisampled_render_to_single_sampled`.
- **Files:** `mali_cmd.c`, `mali_render.c` (**HOT**), `mali_meta*.c` (resolve shaders), `mali_physical_device.c`.
- **Hardware:**
  - Valhall resolves in the tile buffer, which makes render-to-single-sampled the natural fit. The PanVK and blob
    columns show it on all three GPUs.
  - Sample counts: the Arm blob reports 1/2/4/8 (see `framebufferColorSampleCounts` in the matrix).
- **Consumers:** Vulkan 1.2 core, LDB24, Android 16 (`multisampledToSingleSampled` is one of the alternatives), ANGLE
  (MSAA render-to-texture), zink (GL MSAA), DXVK (MSAA resolve).
- **Depends on:** nothing. It conflicts with WP-02 on `mali_cmd.c`, so serialise the two.
- **Tests:** existing `t_vk_feat samplecnt`, plus a new `msaa_load_resolve` case (`driver-msaa.md`). dEQP-VK:
  `renderpass2.depth_stencil_resolve.*`, `pipeline.monolithic.multisample.*`,
  `pipeline.monolithic.multisampled_render_to_single_sampled.*`.
- **Size:** 3 days.
